# Accepted performance ceiling — 2026-10-17

The user accepted the optimized renderer as the new performance ceiling and
requested this confirmation run before committing. These are fresh release
measurements after the three parallel simplify reviews (reuse, quality and
efficiency); all three found no worthwhile changes. Gameplay, authored geometry,
poses, materials, shadow filtering, resolution and weather remain present.

Use these workload-specific observations as the regression reference under the
[performance policy](../../docs/performance.md#accepted-performance-ceiling).
The suite retains its 13 ms mean simulation-step and 25 ms p99 frame budgets,
10% relative tolerance, 15% maximum trial spread, and three repetitions. Acceptance
does not relax those checks or establish that every frame meets 60 FPS.

Use a visible foreground game window, an awake display, no competing game,
companion polling or heavy builds, and leave input alone. Other workload names
come from `benchmarks/suite.json`. The exact commands and full settings are also
retained in the raw results.

Dirty historical evidence is deliberately not automatically reused after source
changes. Decompress the linked workload's `.json.gz` into a fresh ignored
`target/perf/accepted-WORKLOAD.json`, then pass it explicitly with
`--baseline target/perf/accepted-WORKLOAD.json --workload WORKLOAD`. Use ordinary
live evidence for ordinary runs and the headless result for headless runs.
Keep original evidence unchanged; a later commit does not change its provenance.
A noisy reference can yield an inconclusive comparison and cannot be blessed into
stable evidence by this acceptance.
